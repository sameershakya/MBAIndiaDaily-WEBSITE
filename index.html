The Person Behind the Brief

Written by a student,
for students.

MBA India Daily was born from a simple frustration: business news was either too shallow to learn from, or too technical to understand without a Bloomberg terminal. There was nothing built specifically for the MBA student — someone who needs to understand both the headline and the strategic implication behind it.

So I built it myself. Every edition is researched, written, and published daily — connecting global events to India's economy, with a manager's lens on every story.

🎯 Every story has a "why it matters" and a career takeaway — not just facts
🔗 Cross-domain connections that you won't find in any single news source
📅 Published daily — because the market doesn't take days off, and neither do we
